About me

One of the youngest bagpipe makers in the Czech Republic, devoted to an honest craft and to making instruments with a soul.

A craft that found me

I am 34 and have been close to folk culture since childhood. What began as curiosity and admiration for a traditional instrument gradually became a lifelong passion and calling.

I studied double bass at the Conservatory in České Budějovice and later graduated from the Faculty of Education at the University of South Bohemia, qualifying to teach music and Czech.

I founded the Young Bagpipe Band of Strakonice, which has given around 400 performances in its 20 years, in the Czech Republic and abroad—in Brazil, China, South Africa, Scotland and most European countries. As its artistic director, I have arranged many pieces for bagpipe ensembles.

I serve on the programme council of the International Bagpipe Festival in Strakonice and have created many festival programmes.

Last but not least, I am a husband and the father of two wonderful children.
