THE INSTRUMENT

The bagpipe

Every bagpipe made by Strakonický Dudy is custom-built to order. The chanter has a tuning screw for every note. The bag is made from goatskin with an airtight lining. The bass drone can be tuned with a screw or by simply pulling out its lower section. The design without a shortening device and the rounded brass elbows ensure a resonant sound.

Tuning

I make bagpipes in several standard tunings. E-flat major is best for playing with clarinets, while the sharp keys of D major and G major work very well with string instruments. As a soloist with a tenor vocal range, I prefer F major. These pipes can also be made in their corresponding minor keys.
